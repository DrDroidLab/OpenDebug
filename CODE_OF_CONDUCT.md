# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all contributors, regardless of experience level, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, ethnicity, religion, or similar personal characteristic.

## Our Standards

**Positive behavior:**
- Being respectful and constructive in discussions
- Accepting feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what's best for the project and community
- Showing empathy towards others

**Unacceptable behavior:**
- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information
- Spam or off-topic promotion
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers at [opensource@drdroid.io](mailto:opensource@drdroid.io). All reports will be reviewed and acted upon appropriately.
